### Domainwright: the domain_limit ladder.
###
### A vanilla character modifier cannot take a runtime value, so an arbitrary delta is
### expressed as a sum of powers of two. dlc_apply_delta adds exactly the rungs that sum
### to var:dlc_delta, which makes the set unique and the effect idempotent.
###
### Positive range: +1 .. +127 (bits 1..64). Negative range: -1 .. -63 (bits 1..32).
### These are plain character modifiers, so every vanilla system that reads domain_limit
### sees them. See docs/game-internals.md section 2.
