# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Domainwright to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project:

- Being kind and patient with people asking questions about CK3 scripting, which has a steep learning curve.
- Giving and accepting specific, technical feedback — about a trigger's scope, a loc key, a modifier value.
- Assuming good faith when someone's test result contradicts yours, and then working out why.
- Respecting that this is a hobby project maintained in spare time.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, commit messages or the wiki.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sharing or requesting pirated game files, or pushing anyone to do so.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki, and the project board — and
when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, **JoeyHammoth**, through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/JoeyHammoth) or a direct message. Reports are reviewed promptly
and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this code, and will
explain moderation decisions when it is appropriate to do so.
